### TREE – Plant Growth Monitoring Project

This project was developed to study and monitor plant growth, using clover as the experimental plant. It was created during my second year of university.

The idea came from my personal experience. I often forgot to water my plants, even though I enjoyed observing how they changed and grew over time. This inspired me to develop a system that could help solve this problem while allowing me to study plant growth.

TREE is also my second-year final project. The system stores collected data in an SQLite database for further analysis. It evaluates the plant's growth rate by measuring the amount of green area in captured images and comparing it with previous images to determine how much the plant has grown over time.
